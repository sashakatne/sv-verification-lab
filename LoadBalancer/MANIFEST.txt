Notes / caveats
---------------
- A formal input assumption (m_policy_legal) constrains the config port to the
  three defined policy encodings; the reserved 4th encoding (2'b11) is never
  driven and is out of scope. The UVM driver satisfies this by construction.
- p_rr_bounded_fairness is a bounded-safety (pointer-advance) reformulation of
  the round-robin no-starvation liveness claim, and is clocked on posedge (the
  edge the pointer registers on); all other properties sample negedge to match
  the driver's posedge-NBA stimulus and the UVM monitor.
- Committed evidence is curated: the formal log tails (formal/logs/*.log with
  their EVIDENCE_VERDICT lines), the two transcripts, and the rendered
  artifacts. Raw UCDB/wlf databases are not tracked.
